## ✅ Our Solution

We analyze your payment flow and optimize it using three simple strategies:

### 1. Smart Payment Routing
Not all payment methods cost the same. We route transactions to the cheapest option that still works for your customer.

**Example:** A $500 purchase on credit card costs you $14.80 in fees. The same purchase via ACH bank transfer? $0.50.

### 2. Volume-Based Rate Optimization
Bigger businesses get better rates. We help you qualify for—and actually receive—the rates you deserve based on your volume.

**The Math:** Our proprietary formula reduces effective rates as volume increases:
```
Your Rate = Base Rate × (1 ÷ (1 + log₁₀(volume) ÷ 1.618))
```

At $5M volume, a 2.9% rate becomes effectively **~1.1%**.

### 3. Payment Mix Optimization
We analyze which payment methods your customers actually use vs. which ones cost you the most, then help shift the mix toward lower-cost options without hurting conversion.
